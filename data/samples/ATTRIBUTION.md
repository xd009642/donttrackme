# University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples

Recordings in `iowa/` come from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios
Musical Instrument Samples collection:

<https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html>

The collection was created and directed by Lawrence Fritts, Director of the
Electronic Music Studios and Professor of Composition at the University of Iowa.
The collection page credits the following staff for contributions to recording,
editing and management:

Matt Hallaron, Scott Adamson, Eric Durian, John Ritz, Joss Nichols, Michael Cash,
Nik Francis, Andrew-Struck Marcell, Tohm Judson, Jean-Paul Perrotte, Shane Hoose,
Brian Penkrot, Zach Zubow, Will Huff, Daniel Frantz and Jonathan Wilson.

The collection states that its recordings may be downloaded and used for any
projects without restriction. This attribution is included in appreciation of the
people who created and maintain the collection.

Individual instrument pages contain additional performer, technician, instrument
and recording information. The download script records each page and source asset
URL in `iowa/sources.json`; preserve that manifest when distributing the samples.
